# **App Name**: FeastFast

## Core Features:

- Restaurant Listing & Filtering: Users can browse a list of available restaurants and filter them by cuisine type, delivery time, or ratings to find desired options quickly.
- Dynamic Menu Display: View detailed menus for each restaurant, complete with food item descriptions, prices, images, and customization options.
- Shopping Cart Management: Users can add, remove, and modify the quantity of food items in their virtual shopping cart before placing an order.
- Secure Checkout Process: A streamlined checkout flow for users to review their order summary, enter delivery details, and securely complete their purchase.
- Basic Order Status Tracking: Customers can monitor the real-time status of their placed orders, from 'preparing' to 'out for delivery' and 'delivered'.
- User Authentication & Profiles: Allow users to create accounts, log in securely, and manage essential profile information and delivery addresses.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary brand color: A vibrant, appetizing orange (#E27E36) to evoke energy and hunger satisfaction, balancing warmth with modern appeal. Chosen as a versatile, active color for key elements.
- Background color: A subtle, light creamy beige (#F5F2F0) with a warm undertone. This hue is a desaturated variant of the primary color, creating a comfortable and spacious canvas for content in a light theme.
- Accent color: A deep, rich red (#CF1726) analogous to the primary. This provides strong contrast for important call-to-action buttons, alerts, and highlights without being jarring.
- Headline and body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its modern, highly readable, and neutral aesthetic. Its clean lines and robust family make it suitable for both headlines and detailed menu descriptions, ensuring clarity across the application.
- Utilize modern, clean, line-based icons for navigation, restaurant categories, and cart actions, maintaining a consistent and intuitive user experience.
- Implement a clear, card-based layout for restaurant listings and menu items to ensure scannability and ease of selection on various devices.
- Incorporate subtle, smooth transition animations for adding items to the cart and updating order statuses to provide positive feedback and enhance user engagement.
